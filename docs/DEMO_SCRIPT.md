# 3-minute demo script

The dashboard opens on a 5,000,000 KRW monthly budget. Its request-count
baseline, modeled need, provider supply, service prices, and allocations are
synthetic. Explain provenance before describing an outcome.

1. **Question (0:00–0:25)** — Ask: “기록이 적으면 정말 수요가 없는 걸까요?”
   Show the map and point to the `? 조사 필요` markers. State that population,
   household counts, facility anchors, and cached Kakao road routes are public
   or provider data; operating requests and supply are simulated.
2. **Request-count baseline (0:25–0:55)** — At 5,000,000 KRW, the request-count
   comparison includes 0 of 8 survey-required areas. Balanced includes 5 of 8.
   Both allocate 21 modeled monthly service rounds.
3. **Efficiency vs Balanced (0:55–1:35)** — Efficiency includes 3 areas;
   Balanced includes 8 and five survey-required areas. Explain the modeled
   trade-off: Balanced spends 183,732 KRW in road travel versus 49,589 KRW for
   Efficiency (a 134,143 KRW increase) and uses 8,417 versus 2,397 travel
   seconds. This is a policy trade-off in a simulation, not a measured effect.
4. **Minimum service guarantee (1:35–2:20)** — Move the budget to 4,000,000 KRW.
   The view shows 14 / 16 service areas, 608,959 KRW additional need, and a
   4,608,959 KRW modeled requirement. Move the slider to 5,000,000 KRW: it
   reaches 16 / 16.
5. **Close (2:20–3:00)** — Repeat that the current public-data pilot is
   Hongseong-gun Janggok-myeon and that the live population download contains
   Chungcheongnam-do rows only. Resident demand, provider schedules/capacity,
   prices, and operating conditions need field validation before real use.

The experiment report includes a 21-seed synthetic sensitivity check; do not
present it as proof of real-world effectiveness. Keep `.env`, secret settings,
facility contact fields, and real resident records out of the recording.
